SalderingGids

Site summary
- SalderingGids is a Dutch topical site about the salderingsregeling, the 2027 policy change, terugleververgoeding, thuisbatterij decisions, energy contracts, and household solar economics in the Netherlands.
- Primary audience: Dutch households with zonnepanelen, EV owners, and readers comparing solar payback before and after 2027.

Editorial facts
- The Dutch salderingsregeling stops on January 1, 2027.
- Households can still salderen through December 31, 2026.
- Until January 1, 2030, suppliers must compensate excess exported electricity at a minimum of 50% of the bare supply tariff.
- The 2030 date is not the stop date for salderen itself.
